# Support, collaboration and professional opportunities

Superior MI Studio is being built as an independent research-and-development effort. Support that helps the project directly includes architecture reviews, Python/Qt engineering, native Linux or Windows testing, UI/UX research, documentation, screenshots, hardware for build/test systems, and introductions to people interested in developer tooling.

**Financial and equipment support:** If you would like to sponsor engineering time, donate equipment or discuss organizational backing, contact **superior.mi.labs@gmail.com**. Please do not send money to an unverified account or assume a donation is tax-deductible; no public donation channel or charitable status is claimed here.

**Paid development work / contract opportunities:** The developer behind Studio is also open to relevant contract work and side gigs in Python, automation, tooling, AI integrations and application development. Send the project scope, expected timeline, remote/on-site requirements and budget range to **superior.mi.labs@gmail.com**.

**Contributor or community-maintainer support:** Open an issue with a short description of your skills, available time and the area you'd like to help. Technical changes must respect `CONTRIBUTING.md` and the repository's architecture rules.

Public repository issues are for technical discussion. For offers involving payment, confidential information or personal details, use email.
